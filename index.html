Glimpse

A person types a single sentence and steps back. Two language models take that sentence and begin talking — to each other. Each utterance is drawn at a random position, one on top of another, in two complementary colors. It can be seen. It cannot be read. The person watches. Interaction, but not interaction.

The prompt is the moment of release: one gesture of initiation produces an autonomous exchange the initiator cannot follow, redirect, or re-enter. This language — for whom is it?

Scan the code, write one sentence, and watch.
